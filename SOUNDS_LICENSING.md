# Sounds Licensing & Attribution (音频许可与开源致谢)

All audio assets included in **SaltAmbience (WhiteNoise-Android)** are curated from respected open-source and open-access sound repositories:
- [Blanket](https://github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket) by Rafael Mardojai (Linux GNOME Ambient Sound Player)
- [Moodist](https://github.com/remvze/moodist) by MAZE (remvze)
- [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/)
- [Freesound.org](https://freesound.org/) & [SoundBible](https://soundbible.com/)
- Self-developed DSP algorithms by SaltAmbience

All raw audio recordings have undergone sample-accurate equal-energy cross-fade processing (1.5s cosine/sine window) to guarantee seamless, non-stop ambient looping.

- These three edited audio files are licensed to the public under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC BY-SA 3.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) license.
- In accordance with Creative Commons compilation doctrine, this requirement applies **solely to the derivative audio files themselves**, and does NOT extend or "infect" the Android Kotlin application codebase or other independently licensed tracks.
